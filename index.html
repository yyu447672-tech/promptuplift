How are these checks decided?

Checks use English/Chinese keywords and simple patterns, so they may miss valid wording or count irrelevant mentions. Task, context, audience, role, and example need explicit wording to pass; weaker clues earn partial credit. Format needs both a format and a length; constraints need both a boundary and a tone. Specific wording passes when there are no listed vague phrases and at least 20 non-space characters; one vague phrase or shorter text earns partial, while two or more vague phrases earn zero. Recognized placeholders, such as [To fill: ...] or [your background here], and templates made only of square-bracketed hints do not earn points. Always review the suggestions yourself.

      A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

      Good to know.

      What does this AI prompt optimizer actually do?

      PromptUplift checks the structure of your prompt with local JavaScript rules. It looks for a task, context, format and length, audience, constraints and tone, a role, an example, and specific wording. It then organizes the original text into Task, Context, Format, and Constraints, with placeholders for missing details. It does not use a generative AI model.

      Is it free? Do I need an account?

      Yes, the tool is completely free. There is no account, subscription, API key, or usage quota. You can also use the downloaded files offline.

      Is my prompt uploaded or saved?

      The tool never uploads or saves your prompt. Analysis and restructuring happen in your browser’s memory. There are no analytics, external fonts, or third-party scripts. Refreshing the page resets the tool; your browser may independently restore form contents. When hosted, the hosting provider handles ordinary page requests, but the tool does not send it your prompt.

      How is the score out of 100 calculated?

      The eight weights are 20, 15, 15, 10, 10, 8, 8, and 6, adding up to 92. A pass earns the full weight, a partial pass earns half, and a missing item earns zero. The total is divided by 92, multiplied by 100, and rounded to the nearest whole number. The score measures signals of structure, not truth, safety, or the quality of a future AI answer.

      Does it support Chinese and work with any AI tool?

      It checks English and Chinese prompts. The page starts in English; the switch changes interface text only. The optimizer estimates the input’s main language from the text and keeps original wording, including mixed-language terms. You can copy the result into any AI tool that accepts text prompts; PromptUplift has no integration with those services.

      Will one-click optimization invent missing details?

      No. It groups your existing wording and adds clearly marked placeholders such as [To fill: target audience]. It cannot understand every nuance, resolve contradictions, or replace vague wording with facts it does not know. Review the arrangement, move any misplaced text, and fill the placeholders before use.
